Ghar Hisaab – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Ghar Hisaab ("the app") is a daily home diary for milk, naan, maid and other home services, committees (BC) and udhar records. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data you enter stays on your phone

Everything you type into the app — item names, rates, daily entries, payments, committee members, udhar records, names and phone numbers — is stored only on your device. We do not run a server and we do not collect, upload, sell or share this data. If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted unless you made a backup.

Backups and sharing

When you tap Backup data, Share bill, PDF bill, Share status or a WhatsApp reminder, the app creates a file or message and hands it to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp, Gmail or Google Drive). What happens next is controlled by that app and its privacy policy.

Notifications

With your permission the app schedules local reminders (daily diary reminder and committee collection day). These are created on your phone and are not sent through any server of ours.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, device model and app interaction data. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Children

The app is meant for adults managing household accounts and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
